Cloudflare
Cloudflare is a company that services and maintains digital internet infrastructure across the globe. Because of this, they also provide services we're particularly interested in, such as DNS registrars, static site hosting, image/video storage, and many others of that variety.

The user interface and user experience is simple and clear: access to different settings and functions is clearly labeled. In the event that something may be unclear, their online documentation and blog is accessible and digestible for the average layman. 

The services they provide are robust, dependable, and future-proof because a sizable majority of the internet flows through their channels. Simply put, cloud security and internet infrastructure is their domain. It's serious stuff.
